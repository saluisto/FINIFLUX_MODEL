Radon river tools

Calculators for planning and interpreting radon surveys in rivers and streams: sampling spacing, gas exchange, degassing at weirs and mixing at confluences.

Sampling length Length until 37% of radon is still in the river, and the ideal sampling length (half of it). Degassing models by reach Radon gas transfer velocity k (m/d) per reach from Bennett & Rathbun, O'Connor & Dobbins and Raymond eq. 7. Weir and waterfall degassing Gas transfer velocity k (m/d) across a weir or waterfall from radon measured before and after it. Confluence mixing Fully mixed radon concentration (Bq/m³) where two rivers or streams join.
All tools run in the browser. No data is sent anywhere.
