Model equations (as in Gas_exchange_equations_Laos.xlsx)
Bennett & Rathbun (1972): v in ft/s (v·3.28), d in ft (d·3.28), converted from O₂ k₆₀₀ [1/d] = 106.16 · v_ft^0.413 · S^0.273 / d_ft^1.408 · k_Rn [m/d] = k₆₀₀ · (960/500)^-0.5 · d
O’Connor & Dobbins (1958): v in m/s, d in m, gives radon directly k_Rn [1/d] = 9.301·10⁻³ · (v·86400)^0.5 / d^1.5 · k_Rn [m/d] = k_Rn [1/d] · d
Raymond et al. (2012), eq. 7: streams and small rivers, converted from CO₂ k₆₀₀ [m/d] = 4725 · (v·S)^0.86 · Q^-0.14 · d^0.66 · k_Rn [m/d] = k₆₀₀ · (960/600)^-0.5
