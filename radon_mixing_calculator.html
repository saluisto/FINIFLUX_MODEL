Where the mix falls between the two end-members

The mixed concentration always lies between C₁ and C₂. It sits closer to the river that carries more water.

Linear mixing model

C_mix = (Q₁·C₁ + Q₂·C₂) / (Q₁ + Q₂)

The radon flux of each river (Q·C, in Bq/s) is conserved through the confluence and spread over the combined discharge.

The example values are for illustration only. The model assumes complete mixing and no radon loss or gain at the confluence. Degassing and decay between the confluence and the next sampling point are not included.
