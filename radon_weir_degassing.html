How k is calculated

Steady-state radon transport along the reach, with no groundwater inflow:

Q · dC/dx = −k · w · C − λ · d · w · C

Separating variables and integrating from C(0) = C₀ gives

C(x) = C₀ · exp( −(k·w + λ·d·w) · x / Q )

Setting x = L and solving for k:

k = Q / (w · L) · ln( C₀ / C_L ) − λ · d

With Q in m³/s, λ in s⁻¹ and lengths in m, k comes out in m/s. The tool multiplies it by 86 400 to report m/d.

The example values are for illustration only. The result assumes no groundwater or tributary inflow between the two samples, and constant width and depth. λ = ln 2 / 3.8235 d. Because the distance across a weir is short, small errors in C₀ or C_L change k strongly: the result scales with ln(C₀ / C_L).
